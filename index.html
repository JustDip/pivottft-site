PivotTFT

A companion app and passive overlay for Teamfight Tactics.

About the App

PivotTFT is a desktop companion app and passive in-game overlay designed to help Teamfight Tactics players study meta compositions, research opponents outside of active matches, and review their post-match performance to improve their gameplay.

The desktop window provides reference data including a meta comp tier list, an items cheat sheet, a static positioning board, a champions and traits reference, and a team builder. It also features a "Lobby Scouter" tool used strictly outside of an active match (for pre-game research or post-match review) to view opponent ranks and recent matches.

The desktop app includes a comprehensive Match History tab. After a match concludes, the app queries the Riot Match-V5 API to display placements, LP changes, final boards (player and opponents), augments, and traits. This is combined with local game-events captured silently during the match (via the Overwolf SDK) to provide a post-match Timeline view, Round Detail view, and Shop RNG Analysis.

To ensure competitive integrity, the in-game overlay functions solely as a passive reference tool. Users can "Pin" a meta composition in the desktop app before a match. During the game, the overlay only displays that static, pre-selected comp (target star levels, recommended carry items, static positioning, and traits). The overlay contains no live game state, no dynamic recommendations, no win-rate percentages, and no active opponent scouting.
